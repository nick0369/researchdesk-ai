Coverage boundaries

Infosys is the implemented issuer adapter. Additional NSE/BSE companies require a reviewed mapping for their financial statements. Uploaded documents are reviewed separately and never treated as validated actuals automatically.

Governance flags are review prompts, not legal conclusions or a clean bill of health. ROE and ROCE use annual earnings and average balance-sheet denominators. EBITDA is derived from Ind AS results and includes exceptional charges.

DCF treats lease liabilities as debt. Forecast capital expenditure includes an assumed allowance for ROU additions; the cash-flow scorecard instead shows reported cash capex. Terminal reinvestment is recalculated at terminal growth. P/E uses reported owner PAT; EV/EBITDA uses the first forecast year. Multiples are scenario inputs, not observed peer comps.
